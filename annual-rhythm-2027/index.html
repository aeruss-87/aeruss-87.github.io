Option A: results wrap Fri Sep 10 (recommended)

Kickoff Mon Aug 2. Leaves two clear weeks (Sep 13 to 24) plus the Q4 OKR Review week before Ryder’s Oct 1 merit close. Manager training Jul 19 to 23 clears both the July 4 holiday week and the Q3 OKR week. Promotion Committee lands on a clean Monday in office. The only overlap is peer feedback inside the August remote week, which is async form completion.

Option B: results wrap Fri Sep 3

Kickoff Mon Jul 26. This is the closest achievable reading of “end of August.” It buys two more weeks of daylight before merit close, but manager training falls inside the Q3 OKR week (Jul 12 to 16), the one week of the quarter that is in office with a Thursday outing and travel for remote staff.

Why these two, and what a literal end of August would cost

Where this came from: leadership asked for the Q3 cycle to be pulled earlier so it clears Ryder’s comp window with room to spare. The reasoning was that if a cycle surfaces a significant comp change, or a conversation needs more time, there should be real room to handle it before merit closes rather than a single week.

Why the old plan failed the test: the August draft offered an Aug 9 or Aug 16 kickoff, wrapping Sep 17 or Sep 24. Aug 16 leaves one week. Aug 9 nominally leaves two, but the second is Q4 OKR Review week with assessments locking Thu Sep 30, so in practice it also leaves one.

A literal end of August is worse than Option B. Wrapping Fri Aug 27 needs a Jul 19 kickoff, which puts manager training in the July 4 holiday week and sends peer requests out on the Friday of the Q3 OKR week. Every week earlier than Option A drags prep one week earlier too, and Jul 12 to 16 is where it breaks.

Q1 gained a week, and one thing had to move with it. The Q1 2026 cycle ran an upward feedback week that the August draft had dropped. Adding it back pushes Q1 from six phases to seven, so the cycle starts a week earlier (360 peer feedback Feb 1 rather than Feb 8) instead of running a week later, which would have wiped out the buffer before Q2 OKR Review. Every phase from self-eval onward keeps its original dates. The knock-on: the Q1 engagement survey has to move, from Jan 25 to Feb 5 (which would now still be open at kickoff) to Jan 19 to Jan 29. Upward feedback is Q1 only and is not run in Q3.

Still open on this page: the March remote week is shown at Mar 8 to 12 as drafted, but it is decision 6 and not settled (Mar 22 and Mar 29 are live alternatives). December is shown as two remote weeks, which is decision 1. The holiday bands assume Baton sets its own list, which is decision 7.
