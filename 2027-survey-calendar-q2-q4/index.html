Deep surveys in April and October, pulses in January and July

Compare with Option A (deep surveys in Q1 and Q3). Same windows, same seven steps; only which surveys go deep changes. Two deep surveys (28 items) and two pulses (7 or 8 items). Each one gets findings to SLT, a share-out to the team, and a "you said, we did" update at the next OKR Week kickoff. The muted lanes are the 2027 rhythm the dates are built around.

The seven steps every survey follows

  1. OpenMonday. Stays open two weeks.
  2. First reminderDay 3 or 4, the Thursday of week one.
  3. Last callThe final Thursday.
  4. CloseFriday of week two.
  5. Findings to SLTAbout 10 business days after close. SLT risk rows updated within a week.
  6. Share with the teamWithin two weeks of the SLT readout, never in a perf results week.
  7. You said, we didAt the next OKR Week kickoff. It gates the next survey.

What changes compared with Option A

Deep surveys skip the perf quarters

Q2 and Q4 have no perf cycle, so the 28-item survey never competes with peer reviews or self-evals. The pulses take those slots instead.

Full findings reach both SLT offsites

April findings land before the May H2 planning offsite and October findings before the November offsite on 2028 goals. In Option A, both offsites get pulse data.

It continues from Q4 2026

Q4 2026 was a deep survey, so a January pulse is the natural check on its actions, and deep surveys sit six months apart from the start. Option A runs two deep surveys three months apart.

Lighter analysis during perf

Deep-survey analysis and readouts fall outside the Q1 and Q3 perf cycles that People Ops is running.

Deep surveys follow perf results

April opens about 6 weeks after promo results, so reactions to results show up in the deep read. Option A closes both deep surveys before results go out.

October may overlap merit letters

If merit letters land in mid-October, the deep survey catches the reaction. Confirm the timing before locking the window.

No full read until April

Early-year changes show up only in the pulse's action checks until the April survey.

The rules behind the dates

Two weeks, with an early reminder

In 13 waves from Jul 2025 to Sep 2026, responses came in two bursts, at launch and in week two. Moving the first reminder to day 3 or 4 should pull the second burst forward.

Test it on the January pulse

If 80% or more of the final count is in by day 7, the July pulse can run one week. If not, two weeks stays.

No update, no next survey

If the "you said, we did" update isn't ready for the OKR Week kickoff, the next survey doesn't open.

Clear of the perf load

Deep surveys run in Q2 and Q4, the quarters with no perf cycle. The 2-minute pulses take the slots next to the perf cycles, and no team share lands in a perf results week.
