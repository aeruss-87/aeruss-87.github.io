Q1 annual review

Full 360 peer round and upward feedback. Feb 1st to Mar 12th, with prep the week of Jan 25th.

Q3 midyear check

Peer feedback for promotion candidates only. Aug 2nd to Sep 10th, with prep the week of Jul 26th.

  • Annual goals: pods send broad strokes by Fri Nov 20th, SLT offsite the week of Nov 30th, goals locked Fri Dec 4th. Company OKRs go to teams Mon Dec 14th, team drafts are due Mon Jan 4th, the leadership read is Tue Jan 5th, and final OKRs land in Jira Fri Jan 8th.
  • SLT offsites: the week of Nov 30th, 2026 for 2027 annual goals (timing being set in #ash-slt), Tue Jul 27th to Thu Jul 29th, 2027 at midyear, and Tue Nov 16th to Thu Nov 18th, 2027 for 2028 annual goals, which keeps it clear of Thanksgiving, the holiday party and the Baton Special Holiday.
  • In each prep week managers finish training and send every feedback request by Friday, so week 1 is only for writing.
  • The Promotion Committee meets on the Monday of week 5. Results delivery starts that Tuesday and runs two full weeks.
  • March remote week is drafted for Mar 8th to 12th. Moving it to Mar 29th would match spring break for three families, but that is the week before Q2 OKR Week.
  • Q3 results wrap Fri Sep 10th, three weeks before Ryder's Oct 1st merit close.
