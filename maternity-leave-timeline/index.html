How programs sequence (STD, then PFL)

The key insight: Ryder STD and CA PFL cover different phases of your leave and run back-to-back, not at the same time. This means they can add up to significant coverage.

Phase 1: Disability (pre-delivery and recovery)
Two payers, at the same time. CA EDD pays your state disability benefit, and Lincoln Financial pays the remainder up to your Ryder STD benefit level. These are not either/or, and you must file both claims: the state portion does not arrive on its own, and Lincoln calculates its payment net of what the state pays you.
Duration is not the same for both. Ryder's STD benefit runs 12 weeks regardless of delivery type. The state disability period is what varies: typically up to 4 weeks before delivery plus 6 weeks (vaginal) or 8 weeks (C-section) after. Your doctor certifies the exact period.

Phase 2: Bonding (after disability ends)
CA PFL kicks in after disability ends and runs 8 weeks regardless of delivery type. SF PPLO supplements PFL if you work in San Francisco. Your CFRA job protection also starts here, when state disability ends, and runs 12 weeks. Do not read the 12-week CFRA bar as 12 weeks of PFL pay. CFRA is job protection, not income.

Total income replacement: Up to 12 weeks of Ryder STD during disability, then up to 8 weeks of CA PFL during bonding = up to 20 weeks of some level of pay. In practice, the disability phase is typically 6-8 weeks, so most employees see 14-16 weeks of income replacement total.

For 1+ year at Ryder: state disability plus Lincoln brings you to 100% of pay during disability. During bonding you drop to CA PFL, capped at $1,765/wk.

For under 1 year at Ryder: the same two payers bring you to 80% of pay during disability, then CA PFL during bonding. Pre-existing condition exclusions may apply.

Action steps

As early as possible: Tell your manager. Loop in Laura Forte or Ashley Russell.

6+ weeks before leave: Contact Lincoln Financial and start your CA EDD application. Neither has an early-filing limit, so start them as soon as you can.

About 30 days before leave: File your leave request on MyRyder. Ryder generally will not accept a request filed more than 30 days in advance, so do not plan on filing this one early. Confirm the exact window with Karen Rios.

2-3 weeks before leave: Get healthcare provider certifications. Upload to MyRyder and Lincoln. Submit CA SDI claim.

After birth: File CA PFL bonding claim through EDD. Notify Lincoln of delivery date and type.

Before returning: Contact Ryder and Lincoln 1-2 weeks before return. Get doctor's clearance note. Drug screen required if out 90+ days.
The biggest trap: FMLA approval does not mean you get paid. FMLA is job protection only. You must separately file for STD and SDI to receive income during leave. File all three tracks as early as you can.
